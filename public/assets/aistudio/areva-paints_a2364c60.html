Building a better future through innovation, quality and sustainability

Areva Paints began by supplying texture products directly to construction companies. Today we develop low-VOC and specialised coatings, and work with dealers, builders and contractors across four states.

Our range includes seamless putty, transparent protective coatings, heat-insulating paints, road repair mortars, underwater coatings and advanced textures.

2003
Started manufacturing and supplying texture products to construction companies.
2012
Expanded across Karnataka through a growing dealer network.
Today
Growing into Maharashtra, parts of Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh.

Our vision

To become a trusted name in innovative paints, protective coatings and specialised construction solutions through quality, sustainability and lasting value.

Our mission

To develop reliable, high-performance and environmentally conscious products that solve real construction challenges, with strong partnerships across the trade.
